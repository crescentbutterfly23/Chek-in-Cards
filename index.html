Sheet

After a test print on Avery stock, use the nudge to line the print up with the perforations if your printer feeds a little off. Directions are on the sheet held portrait, as it feeds. Negative numbers move it left or up.

Edges

The sheet has no side margin and most printers can't print the last ~0.17in of the paper, so the dark background would get an uneven white edge. Leave it at 0 to print the badge at its true Avery 8522 size (4.25 × 6in). A value above 0 shrinks the design to sit inside an even white border. The white-background design already keeps its content 0.29in or more from the edges.

Runs the dark background 0.125in past the sheet's outer edges. Where two inserts meet, or a badge meets its tickets, it stops at the perforation. On 8522 it applies only when the white margin is 0.

The thin border from the design. Off by default: on perforated stock a line that lands slightly off the perforation shows.

Only needed for plain paper you cut yourself. Avery stock separates at the perforations.

Builds an exact PDF in this browser, the same in Safari, Chrome or Edge. Open it in Preview or Acrobat and print at 100% / Actual size, not "Scale to fit", on Letter paper.

Print the test sheet on one Avery sheet before the full run. Each insert, badge and ticket gets a line 1/8in inside its perforations, and two rulers must measure exactly 8in and 9in. If the gaps are uneven or the rulers come up short, the printer is scaling the page: set Scale to 100% / Actual size and turn off "Fit to page". An even shift in one direction is what the nudge fixes.

Print from browser works in Chrome and Edge only: Letter, portrait, Margins: None, Scale: 100%, Background graphics on. Safari ignores these settings and adds its own margins, so use the PDF.
