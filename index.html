ASCEND Check-in Cards

For Avery 5392 name badge inserts: 6 per sheet.

Design

Black & white keeps the logo badge in color but drops the dark background, which is where most of the ink goes.

Tickets

One ticket per line: ticket number or ticket number, QR content. Lines without QR content use the rule below. You can paste two columns straight from a spreadsheet.

CSV: any export with a ticket-number column and a link column. The tool finds both columns (other columns are ignored), and each card's QR code opens that row's link.

QR images: if your check-in system gives you one QR picture per ticket, name each file by its ticket number (E1004.png). A matching image is used instead of a generated code.

Or fill a number range
Sheet: Avery 5392

6 per sheet, 2 across × 3 down. Each insert is 4in wide × 3in tall, and the card design is turned sideways to fill it.

After a test print on Avery stock, use the nudge to line the print up with the perforations if your printer feeds a little off. Directions are on the sheet held portrait, as it feeds. Negative numbers move it left or up.

Edges

Runs the dark background 0.125in past the sheet's outer edges. The inserts share their inner edges at the perforations, so they need no bleed there.

The thin border from the design. Off by default: on perforated stock a line that lands slightly off the perforation shows.

Only needed for plain paper you cut yourself. Avery 5392 separates at the perforations.

Print this on one Avery sheet before the full run. Each insert gets a line 1/8in inside its perforations, and two rulers must measure exactly 8in and 9in. If the gaps are uneven or the rulers come up short, the printer is scaling the page: set Scale to 100% / Actual size and turn off "Fit to page". An even shift in one direction is what the nudge fixes.

In the print dialog: Letter, landscape, Margins: None, Scale: 100% (or "Actual size"), and turn on Background graphics. Test one sheet on Avery 5392 stock before the full run.
